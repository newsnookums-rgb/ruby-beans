Tip Rush

Starring Crystal · Ruby Beans

How to play
  • Seat: drag a group onto a free table. A chair keeps the colour of its last guest: seat that colour again for a growing bonus.
  • Drinks: tap a machine, then a table or someone in line, to win back hearts. Their favourite gives more.
  • Order: tap the table, then tap the order wheel on the counter.
  • Serve: tap the plate when the chef rings the bell, then tap the table.
  • Cash up: tap the table for the bill. Tap the coins to grab the tip, then tap again to clear.
  • Dishes: drop dirty plates in the dish tub. Crystal carries two things at once.
  • Babies and dogs: tap the baby & dog stand, tap an empty table, then seat them. Babies need a highchair, dogs a water bowl.
  • Chains: do the same job twice or more in a row for a growing bonus.
  • Worlds: 10 days each at the diner, dhaba, café and chippy, each with a new twist. Then endless.
  • Podium: tap it and Crystal greets the line, so they stop losing hearts.
  • Gravy: the pup lives at Ruby Beans. She naps by the counter and trots after Crystal. Tap her for a cuddle.
  • Upgrades: each new day you clear, pick 1 of 2 upgrades for that restaurant, or 1 of 3 with an Expert score. Every world starts plain.
  • Stars: you have 3. Miss a goal on a new day and lose one. Lose all 3 and that world starts over. Walkouts cost $30.
